My name is Pawel Ngei, I'm a [solarpunk](https://storyseedlibrary.org/), full-stack web developer, cybersecurity educator, open science and free software activist and a former [Warsaw Hackerspace](https://hackerspace.pl/) facilitator.

I used to be fascinated by [transhumanism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transhumanism), but I became disillusioned as I learned that its proponents are interested in growth for the sake of growth, without any critical thoughts on its direction. Right now I'm more closely aligned with [Solarpunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solarpunk) and [hacker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacker_ethic) sensibilities.

I have a background in mathematics, physics, neurobiology and computational neuroscience. I program mainly in TypeScript and Python and have experience in Linux systems administration.

My biggest projects include [Glider Ink](https://glider.ink/), a story explaining hackerspaces and hacker culture, [Solarpunk Prompts](https://podcast.tomasino.org/@SolarpunkPrompts), a podcast for climate fiction writers and the [Story Seed Library](https://storyseedlibrary.org/) - a repository of Creative Commons-licensed climate art.
